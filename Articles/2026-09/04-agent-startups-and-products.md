# Agent Startups & Product Launches — September 2026

## Summary

Beyond the frontier labs, a wave of smaller startups launched products this month that treat AI agents as first-class participants in existing human workflows (messaging, insurance/carrier portals, scheduled business routines) rather than as standalone chat assistants.

## Ando — AI agents as team-chat participants

- **Came out of stealth:** September 24, 2026.
- Product: a team messaging app built to let AI agents participate as **first-class members** of conversations, alongside human teammates, rather than as a bolted-on bot.
- **Funding:** $20M pre-seed/seed raise.
- Notable as an early bet that the "agent" UX belongs inside existing collaboration surfaces (chat) rather than a separate dashboard.

## Strada — browser automation for insurance carrier portals

- Announced a browser-automation capability letting its agents **record and replay workflows** inside web-based carrier portals and legacy systems.
- Targets a vertical (insurance operations) full of manual, UI-only legacy systems with no APIs — a common target profile for browser-automation agents this year (see also "Jev" in `01-model-releases.md`).

## Reinventing.AI — open-source "AI Employee" packages

- **Released:** September 19, 2026, on GitHub under the MIT license.
- **Eight open-source packages** covering **59 scheduled business routines**.
- Designed to run on users' own machines, across **11 different agent harnesses** (i.e., harness-agnostic rather than tied to one vendor's agent runtime).
- Represents a notably open, self-hostable counterpoint to the closed enterprise platforms covered in `03-enterprise-agentic-tools.md`.

## Broader framing

Coverage this month repeatedly returns to one line: autonomous software is moving from impressive demos into daily business operations, but most teams still lack the controls needed to let agents act safely — the same gap that Dataiku's Agent Management and Salesforce's Enterprise AI Harness are explicitly trying to fill.
