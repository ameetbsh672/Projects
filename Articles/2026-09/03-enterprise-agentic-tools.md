# Enterprise Agentic Tooling — September 2026

## Summary

The dominant enterprise theme this month was **governance and control infrastructure for fleets of agents**, reflecting an industry shift from "can AI answer this?" to "can AI complete this workflow safely and reliably?"

## Salesforce: AIforce and Headless 360 (Dreamforce 2026)

- Unveiled at Dreamforce 2026.
- **AIforce:** a new AI interface layer for Salesforce's platform.
- **Headless 360:** positioned alongside AIforce as part of a broader **"Enterprise AI Harness"** — combining data, business knowledge, workflows, and control into a composable architecture that lets AI agents understand and act on business processes.
- Framing emphasizes composability and control, not just raw agent capability — a direct response to the containment failures covered in `02-agent-security-incidents.md`.

## Dataiku Agent Management

- **Announced:** September 24, 2026.
- A standalone governance product that:
  - Inventories AI agents across platforms (not limited to Dataiku's own stack).
  - Tracks business KPIs and technical performance per agent.
  - Tiers agents by risk level.
- Signals that "agent sprawl" inside large organizations has become a real management problem — enterprises now need visibility into how many agents exist, what they can do, and how risky each one is, analogous to CMDB/asset-management tooling for traditional IT.

## Google Agent Development Kit (ADK) for Kotlin

- Achieved **feature parity** with the existing Python ADK.
- Adds **on-device AI support for Android**, extending agent development beyond server/cloud contexts into mobile-native, on-device agent runtimes.

## Google Cloud Startup Summit (September 30)

- Explicitly focused on agentic AI, voice AI, generative media, and security.
- Framed around the same shift noted above: from "can AI answer this?" to "can AI complete this workflow safely and reliably?" — reinforcing that this is now the industry's central open question, not model quality per se.

## Why it matters

Model capability announcements (see `01-model-releases.md`) are increasingly paired with, or overshadowed by, **control-plane products**: inventories, risk tiers, KPI tracking, and composable harnesses. This is the enterprise market's direct response to a month that also produced multiple public agent-containment failures — capability and governance are now shipping in lockstep.
